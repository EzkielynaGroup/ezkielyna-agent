# Plans and models

The models you can use depend on your **key type**. The full list and current prices are here:

- Models and prices: https://ezkielyna.store/models
- Service status: https://ezkielyna.store/status

The app reads the model list from your own key, so what you see in the list is what you may actually use.

| Key | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Standard** | Everyday models from the DeepSeek, GLM, Kimi, MiniMax, Qwen and MiMo families. Includes the **Auto** alias (picks for you). No Claude/GPT. |
| **Premium** | Everything in Standard plus top-tier models such as Claude, GPT, Grok and Gemini. Higher price (about 1.5x). Availability of each model can change, check the models page. |
| **Flash** | Fast, low-cost models for daily work: `deepseek-v4.1-flash`, `minimax-m3.1-flash` (and other Flash variants). The quota follows the plan you bought. |
| **Luna** | A plan for the GPT "Luna" model family. Available in several quota sizes. |

Notes:
- **Auto (Pintar)** only uses models on your key. Flash keys stay on Flash models.
- The table is an overview. What applies to you is what the app shows and what https://ezkielyna.store/models lists.
- If a model has problems, check the status page.
